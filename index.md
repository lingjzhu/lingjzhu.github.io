## About Me

Hi there! I am currently an assistant professor at the [Linguistics Department at the University of British Columbia](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/), an associate member of UBC Computer Science, part of [UBC NLP Group](https://nlp.cs.ubc.ca/), and an instructor for the UBC [MDS-CL Program](https://masterdatascience.ubc.ca/programs/computational-linguistics). 

### Research
My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, speech/language technology, and computational social sciences. The big questions I ask are how AI systems learn and discover structures in unfamiliar languages with limited supervision, and how AI agents can reshape knowledge and culture production across research communities and broader human societies. 

In my current work, I am developing deployable and efficient systems that generalize across world languages, including those with little or no training data (e.g. [ZIPA](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.961/); [CLAP-IPA](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.43/)). Increasingly, I am working on how AI agents can form and test hypotheses about previously unseen languages and environments (e.g. [AutoTypologist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07791); [LingGym](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.69/)). With equal passion, I also study the structure and the dynamics of digital cultures and communities (e.g., [ICWSM paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/42708); [CHI paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3706598.3713877)).

### Before UBC
Before joining UBC, I was a post-doctoral research fellow at [Blablablab](https://blablablab.si.umich.edu/), UMSI. I had obtained my Ph.D. in Linguistics and Scientific Computing from the [Department of Linguistics](https://lsa.umich.edu/linguistics) and the [Michigan Institute for Computational Discovery & Engineering](https://micde.umich.edu/) at University of Michigan-Ann Arbor. During my Ph.D. years, I was fortunately advised by [Pam Beddor](https://lsa.umich.edu/linguistics/people/faculty/tenure-track-faculty/beddor.html) and [David Jurgens](https://jurgens.people.si.umich.edu/).

### For students
If you are a UBC undergraduate or MDS-CL student and interested in working with me, please fill in this [Google Form](https://forms.gle/z7bm9HSBFABRXu4L7). I will reach out to you if our research interests are closely aligned. 

For current PhD students and researchers interested in working together, feel free to connect with me! 

For PhD/MA applicants, if you are interested in working with me on multilingual speech processing, sign language processing, LLM agents for computational social sciences/meta-linguistic knowledge discovery, please consider applying to [UBC Linguistics Department](https://linguistics.ubc.ca/graduate/admissions/) and mention my name in your statement. Students with strong computational backgrounds are particularly welcome. I also occasionally co-supervise graduate students with faculty in the UBC NLP Group, depending on shared research interests and available opportunities.
